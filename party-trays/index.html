Panera Party Trays & Platters: Prices, Serving Sizes & the Tray-vs-Boxed Decision (2026)

Trays feed a party for roughly half the per-person cost of boxed lunches. Here is the math, the lineup, and which tray fits your party: the decision nobody else shows you. The trays are portioned from the regular menu items, so you can check any single sandwich or soup before you scale it up.

Every catering site lists tray names. Almost none divides a tray price by the people it feeds, and that one division is the whole decision. This page does it for every format: sandwich, salad, soup, mac and bakery trays, plus the add-ons and the honest fine print. Prices from our checked menu numbers are marked verified. Tray prices and serving counts change by cafe, so they are labeled typical wherever they appear.

The one decision

Trays or boxed lunches?

Same kitchen, two ways to feed a group. Pick by how the party actually runs, then check the per-person math below. This page is about the shared table; the boxed-lunch page owns the individual format.

A Panera party tray, also called a platter or assortment, is a shared catering format: sandwich halves, salads, or bakery arranged on trays for guests to serve themselves. Trays trade the per-person labeling of boxed lunches for a lower per-person cost, which is why they win on budget for parties.

Trays: the shared table

For grazing and mingling

~$7 to $10 per person

Deluxe Assortment, reported at $70 to $103 for about 10 people.

  • Cheapest way to feed a crowd: one tray replaces ten individual orders.
  • Variety is built in: half sandwiches let guests mix instead of committing.
  • Best for: birthdays, game days, office parties, any standing-up event.
or

Boxed lunches: one per person

For seated and formal

$12.89 to $16.29 per person

Per-person band reported across catering listings for individual boxes.

  • Labeled and separate: each guest gets their own box, no sharing questions.
  • Dietary needs stay simple: one box can be made differently without splitting a tray.
  • Best for: working lunches, trainings, clients, mixed dietary needs.

The math for 10 people: trays run roughly $70 to $103 total against $129 to $163 for ten boxed lunches, so trays save about 40 to 50 percent per person. Trays win on price Boxes win when the meal needs names on it. If your guests will stand, wander and graze, order trays and spend the savings on dessert. The half-sandwich secret

10 halves = 5 wholes, and that is the point

Tray menus count in half sandwiches, and no catering page explains what that means for your guest count. A Deluxe Assortment's 10 halves are 5 whole sandwiches, portioned so 10 guests each take a half and the table gets variety instead of 5 people splitting identical sandwiches.

The unit

1 tray = 10 halves

Count one half per guest when other trays share the table, two halves for a hungry or sandwich-only crowd.

Why halves win

Variety without waste

Guests take two different halves instead of one whole sandwich, so a single tray covers more tastes and fewer half-eaten sandwiches come back.

The fine print

Minimums, fees and lead times, honestly labeled

Minimum orders

Typical: about a $50 minimum with 24 hours notice for groups of 6 to 8, and a $150 minimum with 48 hours notice for groups of 15 to 20. Minimums are set by each cafe, not by a national catering policy we could verify.

Delivery

Typical: delivery runs around a $15 fee with roughly a $75 minimum, and both vary by cafe. Pickup removes the fee and the minimum pressure entirely, which is why most party orders are worth collecting yourself.

Lead times

Order 24 hours ahead for small groups and 48 hours ahead for 15 to 20 (typical). For holidays and big game days, order earlier than the minimum: cafes cap how many trays one kitchen can build in a morning.

What arrives

Typical: salad dressings are packaged separately and toppings kept apart so greens stay crisp, and utensils come on request. Say the word utensils when you order, and confirm serving tools for soups and mac.

The one rule

Tray prices are the least standardized numbers in Panera catering. Confirm the tray, the serving count and the total with your cafe when you order, and treat every typical figure on this page as a planning range, not a quote.
