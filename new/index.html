New at Panera: What's Worth Trying This Fall (2026)

Every food site announces the new menu. Nobody tells you what to order. These are the new and limited-time items, each with a price, the numbers, and a verdict: try it, or skip it. New items earn their place against the standing menu they are measured against. On this page, ‘new’ means launched in the last twelve months or back as a seasonal return: fall items launched September 2, 2026, and the pumpkin lineup launched August 19, 2026.

Allrecipes covered the fall drop with descriptions and no numbers. Tasting Table tasted the pumpkin items with no prices. Southern Living listed the summer refresh with no verdicts. This page does the missing step: 20 items verified against the 9-city price menu numbers and Panera's official Nutrition Guide dated September 2, 2026, each judged TRY IT, ONLY-IF or SKIP IT. Four newer names do not have confirmed prices or nutrition yet, so they are listed by name only, with no verdict.

How to read this board: TRY IT means the numbers support the order. ONLY-IF means the item is good under one condition, and the condition is on the card. SKIP IT means the verified numbers argue against it, usually sodium or dessert calories in a meal costume. Verified prices are 9-city averages checked September 30, 2026. Classic · Format

You Pick Two

2 halves your call

Half sandwich plus half salad or soup. Half numbers are estimates and labeled as estimates.

Bowls are a variety play, Pick Two is a control play. A bowl locks you into one flavor at 910 to 1,100 calories. Pick Two lets you pair a light soup half with a protein half and land lower. Choose the bowl when the specific flavor is the point, Carnitas Elote for protein or All-In Veggie for value, and choose Pick Two when you want to steer the total.
