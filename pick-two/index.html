    How it works

    Three steps, no surprises

    You Pick Two has survived every menu refresh for a reason: it is the rare deal where you actually choose.

    1

    Pick any two halves

    Choose any two from half sandwich, cup of soup, or half salad. Same category twice is fine: two soups is a legitimate order. The builder above covers all 33 eligible items. A half sandwich is half of a full Panera sandwich; a half salad is half of a full Panera salad; the soup is the cup size.

    2

    Priced as one combo

    The two halves ring up together as one combo, not as two separate full items. The combo price is set at the cafe and varies by location: check your cafe for the exact figure.

    3

    Plus your side choice

    The combo comes with your side choice. One timing note: this is a lunch menu item. There is no Pick Two at breakfast; the lunch menu starts around 10:30am.
