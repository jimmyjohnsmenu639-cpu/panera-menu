The lightest sandwich is the Mediterranean Veggie (520 cal, $8.12). And one warning you will see flagged all over this page: the hot sandwiches are some of the saltiest items Panera sells.

The sodium warning

One sandwich can hold nearly two days of sodium. The Toasted Italiano packs a 4,110mg sodium bomb: nearly two full days’ worth in a single meal (the daily limit is 2,300mg). It is the saltiest thing Panera sells above the counter.

It is not alone: most hot sandwiches cross 2,000mg. If sodium matters to you, go cold, or pick the Cranberry Walnut Chicken Salad: 970mg sodium with 32g protein. That is the best-balanced sandwich on this page.
