Contact us: tell us what needs fixing

Spotted a price that has moved, a nutrition figure that looks off, or a page that could decide faster? Write to us. This form opens your own email app with your message ready to send, so you stay in control of what leaves your device.

Send a message

Fill this in and press send. Your email app will open with everything filled in: just press send there too. Nothing is stored on this site.

So we can reply. It is included in the email you send, nothing more.

The more specific, the faster we can check it: item name, page name, and the date you saw it help a lot.

Pressing the button opens your email app addressed to our inbox. If no app opens, check that an email app is set up on your device, then try again.

What to write about

Corrections help most

  • Price changes: the item, the price you paid, and your city.
  • Nutrition questions: the item and the figure that looks wrong.
  • Broken links or typos: the page name and where it broke.
  • Ideas: a comparison or guide you wish existed.

When you will hear back

We read everything

We read everything that comes in. Replies usually go out within a few days. Simple corrections often land on the site the same week, with the checked date in the footer updated so you can see the change.

Ordering or app problem?

That one is Panera directly

This guide cannot fix an order, a refund, a gift card, or the Panera app. For anything about an order you placed, contact Panera Bread directly through the app or the official website. We can only help with the information on these pages.
