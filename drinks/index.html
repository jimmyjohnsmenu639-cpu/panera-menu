The question everyone asks

What happened to Charged Lemonade?

Charged Lemonade was a caffeinated lemonade Panera Bread sold from 2022 until May 2024. It is not on the current menu; this box explains what happened and what replaced it.

The short version: Panera sold a lemonade with near-maximum daily caffeine, the risk became impossible to treat as a footnote, and the drink left the menu in May 2024. Here is the factual sequence, without the headline fog.

  1. 2022

    Launched as a caffeinated lemonade

    Charged Lemonade arrived as a plant-based caffeinated lemonade. The large 30 oz size contained about 390 mg of caffeine, close to the FDA's 400 mg daily guidance for healthy adults.

  2. 2023 to 2024

    Lawsuits and tighter controls

    After lawsuits alleging cardiac harm, Panera moved the drink behind the counter and added stronger warnings. This page does not draw legal conclusions from those cases.

  3. May 2024

    Discontinued

    Panera discontinued Charged Lemonade. The cases were settled; the terms were not disclosed.

What replaced it

Energy Refreshers

35 to 48 mg

The verified figures used on this page list four Energy Refreshers at 35 to 48 mg of caffeine. Frescas joined the caffeine-free side at 0 mg. The strategy changed from maximum lift in a lemonade cup to a much smaller, clearer caffeine choice.

Reports in 2025 described Energy Refreshers at roughly 28 mg per 20 oz. Serving size and local labeling can explain differences, so use the local drink label for the cup in front of you. The decision point does not change: the replacement is a low-caffeine drink, not a renamed Charged Lemonade.

Before you join

Sip Club: the drink decision

The useful question is not whether Sip Club sounds like a deal. It is whether the drinks you actually order are the drinks it typically covers.

Typically covered

Self-serve fountain drinks

Typically, Sip Club typically covers self-serve fountain drinks, with one drink every two hours. If that is already your regular Panera habit, the membership has a real use case.

The decision: join for the drink you already repeat, not for a new habit you hope to build.

Do not assume coverage

Espresso bar and smoothies

Typically, smoothies and espresso bar drinks are excluded. A membership bought mainly for lattes, Frozen Javas, or smoothies is solving the wrong problem.

Typically: eligible drinks and terms can change. Confirm the current Sip Club list in the Panera app before joining.
