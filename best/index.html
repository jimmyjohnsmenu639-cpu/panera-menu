Before the list

How we ranked

Short and honest: three tests, applied in order. An item had to pass the first two to make the list at all; the third decides whether it stays.

Test 1 · Value

Protein per dollar

For meals, what does the money buy? Protein and staying power per dollar, with price printed on every pick. The treat and the coffee are judged on their own job, with calories and caffeine shown instead.

Test 2 · Satiety

Will it hold you?

A light pick still has to be a meal. The Chicken Noodle makes the list at 180 calories because its 14g of protein holds the meal together; a 180-calorie side would not. The cookie is not pretending to be a meal: it is ranked as a treat.

Test 3 · No regret

The morning-after test

Comfort picks are allowed, with the calories shown. The French Dipper stays at 1,240 calories because 66g of protein is a deliberate trade, and deliberate is different from accidental.
