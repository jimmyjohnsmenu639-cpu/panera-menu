Panera Hours: When to Go (2026)

There is no single national Panera schedule. Franchises set their own hours, and mall and airport cafes follow their hosts. So this page does the two honest things: it shows the real typical hours with the variance stated plainly, and it tells you exactly when to time your visit and how to check your own cafe in 10 seconds. Once the timing is settled, start with the menu finder to decide what to order when you get there.

Franchise cafes set their own hours

Most Panera locations are independently owned, so the operator picks open and close times. Two cafes in the same town can legitimately differ by an hour.

Malls and airports follow the host

A cafe inside a shopping mall or airport opens and closes with the building. Its hours can look nothing like the standalone cafe down the road.

Holidays compress the day

Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day are full closures. Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve usually run a short day with an early close.

Decisions, not data

Time your visit

Every hours site hands you a table. The useful question is different: given how Panera actually runs, when should you walk in?

Before 11:00

Beat the lunch rush

The line peaks from 11:30am to 1:30pm, when offices empty and drive-thrus stack up. It is the slowest window of the day for a walk-in order.

The move: arrive before 11:00am or after 1:30pm for the shortest wait, or order ahead for pickup and skip the line entirely.

10:30am

Breakfast people: respect the cutoff

Hot breakfast (egg sandwiches, soufflés, oatmeal) ends at 10:30am on weekdays and 11:00am on weekends. Bagels, pastries, and coffee continue all day, but the cooked menu does not.

The move: if you are cutting it close, place the order in the app before the cutoff rather than hoping the line moves fast.

Morning

Go early for the freshest bakery

The bakery case is fullest in the morning. At busy cafes, popular pastries and bagel flavors sell out by the afternoon, and what remains has been sitting since the morning bake.

The move: bakery runs belong in the morning. Need a specific bagel dozen for the office? Order it ahead the day before.

Last hour

Late visits: mind the kitchen cutoff

Many cafes stop taking hot-food orders 30 to 60 minutes before the doors close, even while the cafe is technically still open. A 8:45pm arrival at a 9:00pm cafe can mean coffee and whatever is left in the case.

The move: for a real dinner, arrive at least an hour before the listed close, or order ahead so the kitchen has your ticket before the cutoff.

Holiday hours

Panera on holidays

The strong pattern across every source in our research: two full closures a year, early closes around them, and business as usual otherwise.

Christmas Eve Open, closes early Go earlier in the day. Do not plan a late dinner around it.
New Year’s Eve Open, closes early Lunch and early dinner are safe. Late evening is the risk window.
Other federal holidays Typically open Normal or slightly adjusted hours. A quick check before a special trip is still worth it.

The caveat that matters: franchise cafes can move these days around, and mall or airport locations follow the host building. Holidays are exactly when the 10-second check below earns its keep.
