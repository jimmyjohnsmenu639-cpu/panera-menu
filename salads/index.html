New format spotlight

New: Salad Stuffers

Launched April 2026. A Stuffer is the full salad stuffed into a hollowed Italian roll: same greens, same dressing, no fork, no bowl. Panera built it for the drive, the desk, and the one-hand lunch.

The roll is the only real cost. Against the bowl, it adds roughly 50 to 120 calories depending on the build. Protein dips a little because the roll displaces some salad. What you gain is portability: the salad that stays together.

Green bar is the bowl. Gold is the roll. The Green Goddess barely moves: that is why it wins the format.
