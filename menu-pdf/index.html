Short and honest

Why the other PDFs lie

We checked the ranking results for this search so you do not have to. Three failure modes cover almost all of them, and Panera itself leaves the fourth gap open.

Failure 1 · Stale menus

2018 menus in 2026 clothing

Most ranking PDFs are menus from around 2018, re-uploaded with a new title. Items have been added and removed since then, and prices have moved: a sandwich that read $9 then does not read $9 now. If a PDF does not show its data date, assume it is hiding one.

2018

The vintage behind most top results

Failure 2 · Garbled scans

Garbled scans you cannot trust

Per-location PDFs scraped with optical character recognition mangle the numbers: prices lose decimals, calories merge across lines, and item names break. You cannot plan an order from text that cannot reproduce a price correctly. These files are typed data, not scans.

Failure 3 · Spam domains

Hacked-looking hosts, no source

The rest live on unrelated, hacked-looking domains that host a fake menu page and name no source at all. Panera’s own site offers no clean printable menu to fill the gap, which is why the spam ranks. Every page below names its source and date instead.

Paper, used well

How to print

Four habits that make these files more useful than the PDFs they replace. The stamp check takes two seconds and is the whole point.

1

Print double-sided

The Full Menu (10 pages) prints to 5 sheets double-sided, and the sandwiches, soups, and salads files are one page each: one sheet, done. Single-sided works too: you just carry more paper for the same decision.

2

Keep the cheat sheet to 3 sheets

Print the Nutrition Cheat Sheet (6 landscape pages) double-sided and it lives as 3 sheets in a bag or on a fridge: 247 items with calories, protein, and sodium. That is the sheet to bring to the counter.

3

Take a category sheet to the counter

Deciding between sandwiches? Print the one-page Sandwiches sheet and compare all 20 at the counter, sodium and all. Soups and salads get the same one-page treatment; breakfast takes two.

4

Check the stamp first
