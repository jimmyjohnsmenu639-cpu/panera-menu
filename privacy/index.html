Privacy policy: what we collect is nothing

That is the whole policy in one line. The rest of this page just spells out what nothing means in practice, so there is no guessing.

No accounts, no sign in

You never create an account here, so there is no profile to collect.

This guide has no login, no membership, and no saved preferences tied to you. You open a page, read it, and leave. Nothing about that visit is attached to your name, because we never ask for it. The finder and the comparison tools run in your browser and forget everything when you close the tab.

No tracking and no cookies set by us

We do not follow you around, and we do not set cookies.

We do not run advertising trackers, we do not build a picture of what you read, and we do not set cookies from this site to remember you between visits. We also do not use analytics that identify you as a person. If your browser or device keeps its own history of pages you opened, that history lives on your device under your control, not with us.

The contact form uses your own email app

You send only what you choose to type.

The form on the contact page does not send anything to a server. When you press send, it opens your own email app with your message filled in, addressed to us. You can read it, edit it, or close it without sending. If you do send it, we receive exactly what is in that email: your name, your email address so we can reply, and your message. We use it only to answer you and to fix the page you wrote about.

One outside piece: the map on the locations page

The map belongs to its provider, not to us.

The cafe finder on the locations page shows embedded content from Google Maps so you can search near you. That embedded content is governed by Google’s own privacy policy, not this one. When you interact with the map, Google may receive information under its own terms, the same way it would on any site that shows its maps. Everything else on this site is served as plain pages with no outside content mixed in.

We never sell or share your information

There is nothing to sell, and we would not sell it anyway.

Because this site collects nothing, there is no list to sell, rent, or hand over. The only personal information we ever hold is what you put in an email to us, and that stays in our inbox for the purpose of replying to you. We do not pass it to advertisers or to anyone else.

Questions about privacy

If anything here is unclear, or you want an email you sent us deleted, ask through the contact page and we will sort it out. For how the site itself may be used, see the terms. For what this guide is and is not, see the disclaimer.
