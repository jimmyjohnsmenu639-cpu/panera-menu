Drink subscription

IT DEPENDS

Unlimited Sip Club, monthly

The Unlimited Sip Club is Panera’s monthly drink subscription: unlimited drip and iced coffee, tea, and fountain drinks, one redemption per two hours, capped at 30 drinks a month.

$14.99/mo ÷ $3.01 = 5 coffeesTypical Oct 2026, Sip Club runs $14.99+ per month. At the verified $3.01 drip price, 5 coffees a month to break even. Twice a week puts you ahead. At once a month, you lose about $12.

The August 2026 program changes matter: Sip Club is now capped at 30 drinks per month and one redemption per 2 hours, per The Points Guy and The Krazy Coupon Lady reports Aug 2026. It covers drip and iced coffee, tea, and fountain drinks. It excludes espresso drinks and cold brew, per panerabreadsmenus.com.

Annual $99/yr$8.25/mo → about 3 coffees/mo WORTH IT
Rejoin promo $5/mo2 coffees to break even WORTH IT

The catch: latte drinkers should skip it. Lattes are not in the program, so a latte habit pays twice. The annual price and rejoin promo are per Panera MyPanera Week PR Oct 2024 and are dated, so confirm the current price in the app first. Typical · Oct 2026 · cap Aug 2026

Bakery bulk

WORTH IT

Bagel Tuesday, Baker's Dozen

Bagel Tuesday is Panera’s weekly deal on a Baker’s Dozen of 13 bagels at participating cafes.

~$6.99/13 ≈ $0.54 per bagelRegular single verified $1.98. Thirteen singles cost $25.74. At about $6.99 on Tuesday, you pay roughly $0.54 per bagel and save about $18.75 on 13, if you can use or freeze them.

Reports put Tuesday pricing around $6.99 to $8.00 for 13 bagels, per comicsahoy.com Oct 1, 2026, with livecoupons listing an $8 to $14 range in other markets. The day and price vary by cafe.

The catch: if you only want two bagels, this is a SKIP IT. Twenty cents saved on paper becomes $7 of stale bagels in practice. Slice before freezing and toast from frozen. Typical · Oct 1, 2026 · varies by cafe

Rewards program

WORTH IT

MyPanera, the free program

MyPanera is Panera’s free rewards program: members earn points on spending and redeem them for free items and offers.

Free to join · ~7 to 9% backPer The Points Guy estimate, MyPanera returns roughly 7 to 9% on spend in free items and offers. Cost to join: $0. Break-even: immediate.

MyPanera was revamped in August 2026 to a points system, per The Points Guy and The Krazy Coupon Lady reports Aug 2026. Members also get a birthday reward, typically a free bakery treat or pastry (typical).

The catch: do not chase MyPanera+ on purpose. That status requires about $300 per year in spend for roughly a $15 birthday upgrade, about 5% back that you have already earned more cheaply at the free tier. Join, scan, forget about it. Typical · Aug 2026 revamp

Standing value menu

IT DEPENDS

Mix & Match, $4.99

Mix & Match is Panera’s standing $4.99 value menu: pick any two half sandwiches, half salads, or cups of soup at $4.99 each, plus one free side.

Half Bravo ≈ $5.91 + free sideMix & Match is $4.99 per Panera PR via PR Newswire ~Feb 2026: a half sandwich, half salad, or cup of soup, plus one free side (baguette, chips, or apple). A half Bravo alone imputes to about $5.91 from the verified $11.82 whole, so the menu saves about $0.92 plus the side, no coupon needed.

It needs no code and has no expiry. Whether it beats a full entree depends only on appetite: a light lunch wins, a hungry one does not.

The catch: the side is where the value either lands or disappears. Pick the baguette if you will eat it. If the apple stays in the bag, you paid $4.99 for a half you could have split from a $11.82 whole and shared. Per Panera PR · ~Feb 2026 · no code

Read this before you clip anything

The coupon trap

The coupon sites are not lying, exactly. They are just answering a different question. They tell you a code exists. They do not tell you whether it saves you money, whether it will still work on Friday, or whether you had to spend more to use it. That gap is where trips get wasted.

Trap 1 · Speed

Codes die in days

hotdeals.com timestamps its tests, which is honest, and still shows codes expiring Oct 2 to 3, 2026, days after listing. livecoupons.net lists codes with a last test of January 1, 2026, which is not freshness at all. Treat any code older than this week as a rumor until checkout confirms it.

Trap 2 · Targeting

Many codes are not for you

The strongest offers are often account-specific, first-order only, or tied to a subscription you let lapse. CaterCow's $50 off a first $100+ catering order is real money, for a new customer, once. If the code box rejects it, the deal was never yours.

Trap 3 · The threshold

Never plan a trip around a code

A $5 off $15 code only pays if your order was already near $15. Adding an item to clear the threshold converts a 33% discount into full price plus a cookie. Build the order you want, then test the code. If the code fails, the order should still make sense.

The fix is boring and it works: decide the order first from verified prices, then let the code be a bonus. The two deals in the next section never expire and need no code, which is why they are the base of every smart Panera order.
