Sodium honesty

Soup day = watch the rest of the day’s salt

Even the lightest soup carries 1,570mg of sodium: about 68% of the 2,300mg daily value. The Chicken Noodle is the healthiest soup here by calories and protein, and it still spends two-thirds of your salt budget in one bowl.

At the other end, the Mac & Cheese hits 2,300mg (100% of the day) and the Bacon Mac reaches 2,620mg (114%). No soup on this page is low-sodium: the gentlest, Creamy Tomato, is still 1,110mg. The decision is never “salty or not”: it is how much of the day this bowl spends, and what dinner does about it.

What about the bread bowl?

Honest gap: our menu numbers has no bread-bowl combo nutrition, so we are not printing invented numbers. What we can say from the menu’s structure: a bread bowl makes the soup the meal, vessel included. You are eating the bowl.

The decision: treat any bread bowl as a full meal, not a soup upgrade. If the soup alone would not be lunch, the bread bowl will be: order it when the soup is the main event, and skip it when the soup is a side to something else.
