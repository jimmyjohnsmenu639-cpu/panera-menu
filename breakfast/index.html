Panera Breakfast Menu With Prices (2026)

Panera Bread is a US bakery-cafe chain. Its breakfast menu covers egg sandwiches, frittatas and souffles, parfaits, oatmeal, and the bagels the chain is known for.

38 items with 2026 prices, calories, protein, and sodium. Plus the hours question everyone asks: when does Panera stop serving breakfast, and what can you still get after that? Breakfast is one of 24 categories in our complete Panera menu guide.

The short version: the most protein is the Farmhouse Duo Asiago Bagel Stack (34g, $7.03). The lightest real meal is the Greek Yogurt with Berries Parfait (250 cal, 15g protein, 80mg sodium). And the cheapest breakfast on the menu is a single bagel at $1.98, with more protein than you would expect.

Breakfast hours

When does Panera serve breakfast?

The most-asked question about this menu. These are the typical hours across cafes in our research: your cafe sets its own schedule, so confirm locally.

6:00–10:30am Weekdays Monday to Friday: hot breakfast ends at 10:30am
6:00–11:00am Weekends Saturday and Sunday: hot breakfast ends at 11:00am
All day Bakery & coffee Bagels, pastries, and coffee are sold past the cutoff

Missed the cutoff? After hot breakfast ends, the egg sandwiches stop, but bagels, bakery items, and coffee continue all day: breakfast is not fully over. Hours above are typical, not guaranteed per cafe.

The $1.98 secret

Ten of the eleven single bagels cost exactly $1.98. The one exception is the Everything Protein Bagel at $1.99. That makes a bagel the cheapest breakfast at Panera, and quietly one of the best protein values anywhere on the menu.

The protein plays at that price: the Asiago Everything (15g protein, 370 cal, 7.59g/$) and the Asiago (14g protein, 350 cal, 7.08g/$). Most people walk past them to a $7 sandwich with twice the price and, in some cases, barely more protein.

At $1.98, a single bagel is the cheapest breakfast Panera sells; the Asiago (14g protein) and Asiago Everything (15g protein) are the protein plays at that price.

  • No. The hot breakfast menu (egg sandwiches, frittatas, soufflés, and oatmeal) ends mid-morning. The bakery case is the exception: bagels and pastries are sold all day, so a $1.98 bagel still counts as breakfast at 3pm.

  • Typically 6:00am to 10:30am Monday to Friday, and 6:00am to 11:00am on Saturday and Sunday. Treat that as the usual pattern rather than a guarantee for your cafe, and check your local bakery-cafe before you go: our Locations page can point you at it.

  • The Farmhouse Duo Asiago Bagel Stack, with 34g protein ($7.03, 730 cal, 1,520mg sodium). Right behind it: the Sausage & Egg Asiago Bagel Stack (33g) and the Steak & Wake (32g, at a leaner 520 cal).
