Two different orders

Vegetarian vs vegan at Panera

The words sound adjacent. At Panera they describe two completely different ordering experiences, and mixing them up is how orders go wrong.

You are here

Vegetarian: no meat, no modifications

Vegetarian means no meat, with dairy and egg still on the table. That single difference unlocks most of Panera: cheese sandwiches, creamy soups, Mac & Cheese, egg soufflés, and the whole bakery case. Panera even keeps its own vegetarian menu category. Everything on this page is ordered exactly as served.

The decision: pick from the menu above and order. That is the entire method.

The harder version

Vegan: no animal products at all

Vegan removes dairy, egg, and honey too, and Panera has almost nothing vegan as served. The cheese, the yogurt, and the honey in the bread all have to come off, which turns ordering into a routine: three swaps, said out loud, every time.

The decision: if that is your order, use the modification guide instead of this page.
