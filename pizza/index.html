Panera Pizza: The Flatbread Verdict (2026)

Five flatbreads. Three crowns. Readers say Margherita, editors say Chipotle Chicken and Bacon. Here is the honest verdict, by the numbers. Flatbreads are the dinner corner of the menu we cover end to end.

Panera pizza means flatbread pizza: a thin, oblong flatbread baked at Panera Bread, launched to win the dinner daypart. This guide covers all five flatbreads. Prices and nutrition below are per Panera's published nutrition information and typical cafe pricing, not part of the 9-city average. Confirm in the Panera app.

Every other page ranks Panera pizza one way and stops. The reader poll crowns one pizza, the editor ranking crowns a different one, and neither puts a current price next to the calories. This page settles the argument three ways, so you can order by the job you need the pizza to do. Flatbread pizza launched in 2020 starting at $7.99, and Panera built it to win dinner, not lunch.

The decision nobody makes for you

Pizza or sandwich?

Panera sells both, and the right answer changes with the meal. Flatbread was launched to win dinner. The sandwich still wins lunch. Here is the split, with the one verified number that settles it.

Lunch alone · Sandwich wins

Order the sandwich at lunch

47g protein verified

The Bacon Turkey Bravo gives 47g protein for $11.81, with 860 calories and 2,430mg sodium. Those figures are verified in our menu numbers, checked September 30, 2026.

  • Protein per dollar wins at lunch. A solo lunch needs protein that travels well and prices cleanly, and the sandwich does both.
  • No sharing math. One sandwich feeds one person with nothing left to negotiate.
  • The full sandwich lineup is verified. Every price and nutrition figure on the sandwiches page comes from the same menu numbers.

Verdict: lunch for one, order the sandwich.

Dinner or sharing · Flatbread wins

Order the flatbread at dinner

$10 to $15 per flatbread (typical)

Per QSR Magazine reporting, Panera launched flatbread pizza to win the dinner daypart. It was designed as an evening, shareable order, not as a lunch value play.

  • Built to share. One flatbread plus a salad feeds two light eaters, per reviewer reports. A sandwich never splits that cleanly.
  • Dinner calories make sense here. At 760 to 870 calories per published nutrition, a flatbread is a dinner sized portion.
  • Confirm before you go. Pizza prices and availability are typically, so check the app for your cafe.

Verdict: dinner or a table of two, order the flatbread.

Solo or share?

How far one flatbread actually goes

Reviewer reports agree on the portion, even when they disagree on the ranking. One flatbread is a full meal for one hungry person. For two people, it needs help.

One hungry person

The solo order

1 flatbread is the meal

Per reviewer reports, one flatbread feeds one hungry person on its own. At 760 to 870 calories per Panera's published nutrition, that tracks. Add nothing unless you want a drink.

Two light eaters

The split order

1 flatbread + 1 salad split two ways

Per reviewer reports, two light eaters can split one flatbread when a salad joins it. Each person gets pizza and greens, and the table spends less than two solo lunches.

The value math: a flatbread at $10 to $15 typical plus a salad split two ways beats two sandwiches on price typically, because one pizza does the feeding for both. Two hungry people should not stretch it. Order the flatbread plus a salad, or order two items and eat properly. Confirm your cafe prices in the app, because the pizza side of this math is typical.
