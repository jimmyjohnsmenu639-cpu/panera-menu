Panera Value Duets: Are They Actually a Deal? (2026)

Seven set pairings, two price points, one question: is $6.99 for half a sandwich and a cup of soup really cheaper? We did the math, and the duet costs less than the soup alone. Both halves of every duet come from across the whole menu, where the full portions are priced.

A Panera Value Duet is a set pairing of a half sandwich or half salad with a cup of soup, priced at $6.99 or $8.99; the pairings are fixed. Every other site lists the pairings and stops. None divides the duet price by the protein, none compares a duet with buying the cup solo, and none puts Duets next to the $4.99 Mix & Match menu and You Pick Two. This page does all three. Duet prices and pairings are typical and vary by cafe. Soup and sandwich prices and nutrition are verified against our 9-city menu numbers and Panera’s official Nutrition Guide dated September 2, 2026. Half numbers are estimates because Panera does not publish nutrition for halves.

Three ways to pay less

Duets vs Mix & Match vs You Pick Two

Panera now runs three value formats at once, and no other page compares them. Same kitchen, three different bargains: pick by how much choice you actually want, not by the headline price.

Set pairings Typical

Value Duets

$6.99 to $8.99 per duet

Half sandwich or salad plus a cup of soup, from a fixed list of seven pairings.

  • Cheapest complete lunch on this page, and cheaper than the solo cup.
  • No drink, no side included; both cost extra.
  • Pre-set: swaps at the cafe’s discretion.

Verdict: for the regular who already has a favorite pairing. Pick the Smokehouse duet and stop thinking.

Value menu, Feb 2026 Typical

Mix & Match

$4.99 × 2 = $9.98 plus a free side

Ten half and cup items at $4.99 each, two-item minimum, one free side per order: baguette, chips or apple.

  • You choose both items: half sandwiches, half salads and cups, including Bistro French Onion.
  • Free side included, which no duet offers.
  • Costs $1 to $3 more than a duet for the choice and the side.

Verdict: for the explorer. Pay a little more, pick your own pairing, keep the free side. Deal math is on the deals page.

You Pick Two

  • Half numbers are estimates.

Honest notes

When to skip the duet

The duet is a real deal with real edges. Four situations where the cheaper headline is the wrong order, stated plainly so the deal does not talk you out of the lunch you actually wanted.

Skip if

You want a drink or a side

Duets include neither (typical). Add a drink and a side at menu prices and the gap over Mix & Match, which includes a free side, closes fast.

Skip if

Your pairing is not on the list

Seven pairings, pre-set. If your half and cup are not one of the seven, You Pick Two or Mix & Match will build it and the duet cannot.

Watch out

Sodium rides along

The cup alone brings 1,110mg (Creamy Tomato) to 1,570mg (Chicken Noodle), verified. Add any half sandwich and even the lightest duet lands well past 1,500mg. Fine for most lunches; know it before a low-sodium day.

Skip if

You wanted the whole sandwich

A duet half is half. Full verified sandwiches run $8.08 to $9.93 for the mains above, so a full sandwich plus no soup can cost about the same as the duet. Hungry beats thrifty some days.
