Panera Bagels: Every Flavor Checked, Ranked by the Numbers (2026)

The same $1.98 buys you 190 calories or 430. Here is every bagel, ranked, plus the Tuesday math and the cream cheese pairing nobody shows. Bagels cover the morning here; the rest of the menu is mapped out when you are planning the rest of the day.

A Panera bagel is a single-serve bagel baked fresh daily at Panera Bread, sold individually for $1.98 and in money-saving packs. This guide covers all 11 varieties on the 2026 menu with verified prices and official nutrition, plus pack math and the Tuesday deal.

Every single bagel below costs $1.98, except the new Everything Protein Bagel at $1.99. The price tells you nothing, so this page ranks what the price hides: calories from 190 to 430, protein from 6g to 15g, and sodium up to 800mg in a single bagel. Nutrition is taken from Panera’s official guide dated September 2, 2026, and prices are 9-city averages checked September 30, 2026. Tuesday pricing is labeled typical throughout.

The headline finding

Same price, two different breakfasts

Panera prices every classic bagel at $1.98, so the counter gives you no signal at all. The check does: the lightest and heaviest bagels differ by 240 calories, more than a full cream cheese individual.

The light end · $1.98

Multigrain Bagel Flat

190 cal

6g protein · 420mg sodium. The only Panera bagel under 200 calories, and the gentlest sodium of any bagel on the board. This is the default for a light morning.

The heavy end · $1.98

Cinnamon Crunch Bagel

430 cal

13g protein · 460mg sodium. The sweet crunch topping turns this into the highest calorie bagel Panera sells. It is a good order, as long as you order it as dessert.

The decision: at one flat price, calories are the choice, not the cost. Pick by the morning you want: the Flat keeps breakfast light, the Crunch is the treat, and the eight bagels between them trade protein against sodium. The full ranking is next.

The Tuesday math

The deal, the packs, and the freezer

Singles are the worst way to buy bagels at Panera, and it is not close. Thirteen singles cost $25.74. Every pack undercuts that, and the Tuesday special, where it runs, undercuts the packs. Here is the whole ladder with the labels kept honest.

The Tuesday bagel deal is a weekly Baker's Dozen discount at participating Panera bakery-cafes, with typical pricing that varies by market. Confirm in the Panera app before going.

The cream cheese pairing

The spread changes the order

Nobody ranks the spread with the bagel, which is odd, because the spread can matter more than the bagel you picked. Both individuals cost $1.73. The calories are where they part.

Panera cream cheese is sold in 1.5 oz individuals ($1.73) and 8 oz tubs ($3.78). The spread can add more calories than the gap between two bagel varieties.

The lighter spread · Individual 1.5 oz

Plain Cream Cheese

$1.73 per individual
110 cal3g protein150mg sodium

The default pairing, and the lighter one. On a Multigrain Flat it builds the lightest full bagel order at Panera: 300 calories with 9g protein.

Choose it when: the bagel is the flavor and the spread is just there to hold it together.

The flavor spread · Individual 1.5 oz

Honey Walnut Cream Cheese

$1.73 per individual
130 cal4g protein160mg sodium

Twenty more calories and one more gram of protein than plain, at the same price. On a Plain Bagel it builds a 410 calorie order, nearly a Cinnamon Crunch before the Crunch gets its own spread.

Choose it when: the spread is the point. Price it into the bagel decision, not after it.

  • Lightest full order: Multigrain Flat plus Plain300 cal · $3.71

    190 plus 110 calories, 9g protein. A complete bagel order that stays lighter than most single bagels alone.

  • The balanced order: Blueberry plus Plain400 cal · $3.71

    290 plus 110 calories, 13g protein. Sweet, filling, and still under the Cinnamon Crunch on its own.

  • The protein order: Asiago Everything plus Honey Walnut500 cal · $3.71

    370 plus 130 calories, 19g protein. The most protein a $3.71 bagel order can carry, with 960mg sodium to show for it.

  • The full splurge: Cinnamon Crunch plus Honey Walnut560 cal · $3.71

    430 plus 130 calories, 17g protein. Dessert, priced and portioned like dessert. Split it and it is a reasonable one.

The honest note: the gap between a Plain Bagel (280) and a Sesame (310) is 30 calories. A cream cheese individual is 110 to 130. The spread can outweigh the bagel to bagel gap, so decide the pairing with the same care as the bagel. For a group, the 8 oz tubs cost $3.78 each (plain at 80 cal per 1 oz serving, honey walnut at 90): one tub holds about five individual portions that would cost $8.65 bought one at a time.

Bagels as a vehicle

The bagel is also a sandwich base

Half the breakfast menu is built on these same bagels. The Asiago and Asiago Everything that top the protein ranking are the bases for Panera’s Bagel Stacks, which is why a $1.98 bagel with an egg on it becomes a 30g protein breakfast. This page looks at the bagel on its own; the builds live on the breakfast page.

One ordering note carries over: the sodium you see in the check table is the floor, not the total. Cheese, sausage and bacon stack on top of it, so the bagel choice you make here sets the budget for everything added later.
